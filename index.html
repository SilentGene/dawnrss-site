01

Paper and ink

The interface is monochrome: a paper colour, an ink colour and one grey. Colour never carries meaning — read and unread are told apart by weight, sections by hairlines and white space.

  • Serif type for titles and text, at a comfortable measure
  • Light and dark themes; adjustable text size
  • Phones, tablets and foldables; list and article side by side on wide screens
  • English, 简体中文 and 繁體中文
02

Subscriptions, without fuss

Paste a feed address — or just a website, and DawnRSS finds its feeds. Your subscriptions stay yours: they import and export as OPML.

  • RSS 2.0, Atom, RSS 1.0 and JSON Feed
  • Groups, renaming, per-feed and per-group views
  • Swipe to mark read or to star; optionally mark read as you scroll past
  • Starred articles can outlive the subscription they came from
03

Five highlighter pens

Tell DawnRSS the words you care about and give each a pen. They are marked in headlines, summaries and article text — the one place the app uses colour, because the marks are yours.

  • Yellow, green, pink, blue and orange
  • When the keyword sits deep in a summary, the summary jumps to that sentence
  • Whole-word matching, so “RNA” need not light up “mRNA”
04

Translation and briefings, with your own AI

Connect a service you already have and DawnRSS translates foreign feeds and writes you an editor’s note about what just arrived.

  • Works with OpenAI-compatible APIs, Claude and DeepL — your key stays in the system keystore
  • Translate titles and summaries on sync, or a whole article on demand
  • Briefings after each sync, daily or weekly, with citations that open the articles
  • You set the limits on how much is translated and summarised unasked
05

Local first, private by default

Everything works without an account, and then your data never leaves your device. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking code.

  • Back up to and restore from your own WebDAV server
  • An optional account syncs your feed list and starred articles across devices
  • Delete the account from inside the app at any time
06

Pro, if you want more

Reading, subscriptions, highlighting, backup and bring-your-own-key AI are free. An optional monthly Pro subscription adds what costs money to run.

  • DawnRSS AI: translation and briefings with no API key to set up
  • Read aloud, in natural cloud voices
  • A daily report waiting for you at the time you choose
  • Background sync, and articles kept for good (free keeps unstarred articles for six months)
