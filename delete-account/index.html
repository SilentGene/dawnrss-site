Delete your DawnRSS account

DawnRSS (朝闻道RSS) is an RSS reader by Heyu Lin. Reading works without an account; this page is for people who created one and want it deleted, together with everything stored with it on our server.

In the app

  • Open DawnRSS and sign in.
  • Go to Settings → Account → Delete account, and confirm.

The account and its data are deleted at once.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app or cannot sign in, email linheyu@outlook.com from the address you registered with, with the subject "Delete my DawnRSS account". We delete the account within 30 days and reply when it is done. If you write from another address, we will first ask you to show that the account is yours.

What is deleted

  • the account: your email address, your nickname, your hashed password, and when you signed up and last signed in;
  • the starred articles, feed list and highlighting keywords synced to your account;
  • the Pro subscription record (Google Play purchase token, product and expiry date);
  • the record of how much DawnRSS AI and read-aloud you used.

What is not deleted

  • Feedback you sent us is kept, no longer linked to your account, for at most a year after we have dealt with it.
  • Google Play keeps its own records of your purchases under Google's policies.
  • Nothing on your devices is touched: feeds, articles and settings stay until you uninstall the app. Backups on your own WebDAV server are yours to delete.

Delete some data, keep your account

  • Starred articles: un-star an article in the app and its content is deleted from our server; only a marker without any content remains, to tell your other devices.
  • Feeds: unsubscribe in the app and only the feed's address and a marker remain on our server.
  • Highlighting keywords: remove a keyword in the app and it is removed from our server at the next sync.
  • Usage records are cleared out automatically after about 40 days.
  • Anything else — feedback you sent, the markers above, or specific data you name — email linheyu@outlook.com from the address you registered with, saying what to delete. We delete it within 30 days and reply when it is done.

Your subscription

Deleting your account does not cancel a Pro subscription. Cancel it first in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, or it keeps renewing.

More about how we handle your data: https://dawnrss.wondev.top/privacy/
