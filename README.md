DawnRSS is an RSS reader that feels like a book. Paper and ink, serif type, hairline rules, and nothing else competing for your attention. You choose the sites, blogs and journals you follow; DawnRSS brings their new articles together and gets out of the way.

Its Chinese name, 朝闻道 (*zhāo wén dào*), comes from the *Analects* of Confucius: “Know the way at dawn; die without regret at dusk.”

## Features

**Focused reading**
- A calm, monochrome design: read and unread are told apart by type weight, never by colour
- Serif titles and text at a comfortable measure; light and dark themes; adjustable text size
- Phones, tablets and foldables, with list and article side by side on wide screens
- Swipe to mark read or to star; optionally mark articles read as you scroll past

**Subscriptions without fuss**
- Paste a feed address, or just a website: if the site offers a feed, DawnRSS finds it
- RSS 2.0, Atom, RSS 1.0 and JSON Feed
- Groups, renaming, per-feed and per-group views
- Import and export your subscriptions as OPML

**Five highlighter pens**
- Give the words you care about a pen each: yellow, green, pink, blue or orange
- They are marked in headlines, summaries and article text, and a summary jumps to the sentence that mentions them

**Translation and briefings with your own AI**
- Connect an OpenAI-compatible API, Claude or DeepL; your key stays in the system keystore
- Translate titles and summaries automatically, or a whole article on demand
- An editor’s note on what just arrived (after each sync, daily or weekly), with citations that open the articles

**Private by default**
- Everything works without an account, and then your data stays on your device
- No ads, no analytics, no tracking code
- Back up to and restore from your own WebDAV server
- An optional account syncs your feed list and starred articles across devices

**DawnRSS Pro** is an optional monthly subscription that adds DawnRSS AI (translation and briefings with no API key to set up), read-aloud in natural cloud voices, a daily report every morning, background sync, and keeping articles for as long as you like.

DawnRSS speaks 13 languages: English, 简体中文, 繁體中文, 日本語, 한국어, Español, Français, Deutsch, Português, Italiano, Русский, Tiếng Việt and Bahasa Indonesia.
